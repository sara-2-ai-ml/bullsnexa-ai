# CAMPAIGN_REQUIREMENTS_V1 — Campaign requirements

> **SYNTHETIC DEMONSTRATION MATERIAL. NOT AN OFFICIAL BULLS VISION GROUP POLICY OR LEGAL GUIDANCE.**

A launch-readiness check needs structured campaign identity, objective, audience, ISO alpha-2 target country, channels, positive budget and supported currency, ISO calendar dates, KPI, and category. Missing critical information requires clarification. Equal or reversed dates are a hard blocker; a past start date requires human review.

The runtime source of truth is `src/domain/rules-config.ts`. This document cannot override typed configuration.
