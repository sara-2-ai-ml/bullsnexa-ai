# PRIVACY_REVIEW_POLICY_V1 — EU lead-generation review

> **SYNTHETIC DEMONSTRATION MATERIAL. NOT AN OFFICIAL BULLS VISION GROUP POLICY OR LEGAL/PRIVACY GUIDANCE.**

For this prototype only, a campaign with objective `leads` and a target country in the versioned EU code list requires human privacy review. The campaign status is `NEEDS_REVIEW` unless a separate hard blocker makes it `BLOCKED`.

This is a conservative internal review trigger. It is not a claim that every EU lead-generation campaign violates privacy law. The runtime source of truth and EU list are in `src/domain/rules-config.ts`.
