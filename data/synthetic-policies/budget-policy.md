# BUDGET_POLICY_V1 — Budget review

> **SYNTHETIC DEMONSTRATION MATERIAL. NOT AN OFFICIAL BULLS VISION GROUP POLICY OR FINANCIAL GUIDANCE.**

Budgets must be positive, finite numbers in USD, EUR, or GBP. A supplied zero, negative, non-finite, or non-numeric budget is a hard blocker. Missing budget requires clarification rather than being interpreted as zero. A valid budget above 100,000 currency units requires human review but retains its budget score.

The runtime source of truth is `src/domain/rules-config.ts`.
