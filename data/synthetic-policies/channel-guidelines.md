# CHANNEL_GUIDELINES_V1 — Supported channels

> **SYNTHETIC DEMONSTRATION MATERIAL. NOT AN OFFICIAL BULLS VISION GROUP POLICY.**

Phase 1 recognizes `search`, `social`, `display`, `email`, and `video`. An unsupported channel requires review because no deterministic rule exists for it. Structured feedback lists only the unsupported channel names in sorted order and asks the caller to remove or replace them. This does not claim the channel is universally unsafe or unavailable.

The runtime source of truth is `src/domain/rules-config.ts`.
